# Hi, I'm Ledi

AI-native builder and creator, based in Athens.

My agents read messages, file data and draft what to say next.

## What I'm building

- **[message-intel](https://github.com/LedionBrati/message-intel)**. As a creator, a lot comes through my WhatsApp and Instagram and things slip. An agent captures every message, files it into the right place in Notion, and turns it into what I need to do today.
- **gtm-job-hunter**. For creators who want to work at YC startups. It finds new YC companies with a weak social presence and drafts a message to the founder offering to fix it.
- **[x-copilot](https://github.com/LedionBrati/x-copilot)**. A Chrome extension that drafts X posts and replies in your voice. You steer it by tapping chips, not typing prompts.
